# Cargo.lock delta (classified)

`Cargo.lock` was regenerated by Cargo, never edited by hand:
`cargo update -p wasmtime@43.0.2 --precise 36.0.17` after the workspace pin
changed (`lockfile/cargo-update.txt`, Cargo's own report). A first attempt,
made before the pin edit was applied, was refused by Cargo
(`^43.0.2` cannot select 36.0.17) and left `Cargo.lock` byte-identical.

| Before (`a7001311`) | 1092 | `a50216382d813af53e272bd2c56e818adf59993481cc8f9aade25dfd67a53d69` |
| After | 1092 | `3a0e1c7bec333b295eeba3b82e6d816d1b3a64f9d3deda8ee57aed815b0bceb8` |

`scripts/lock_delta.py` compares the two by name and version and lists every
affected package's dependents in the lockfile where it appears
(`lockfile/lock-delta.json`, `lockfile/lock-delta.txt`). It finds 46 removed
and 46 added (name, version) pairs. No package kept its version with another
checksum or source.

## Security relevance

- Removed: Wasmtime 43.0.2 and its four advisories (RUSTSEC-2026-0222, -0269,
  -0316, -0327). Also removed: the unmaintained `bitmaps`, `im-rc` and
  `sized-chunks` (RUSTSEC-2026-0247, -0250, -0251), and `sized-chunks`'
  unsoundness warning (RUSTSEC-2026-0255).
- Added or moved: no advisory in the fresh database matches any added or
  moved package. The fresh scanners report no finding in them
  (`matrices/security-gates.md`).
- `toml` 0.8.23 and `proc-macro-crate` 2.0.0 are build-time parsers
  (configuration and proc-macro crate lookup). The full workspace build,
  Clippy and tests pass with them (`matrices/validation.md`).
