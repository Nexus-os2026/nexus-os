## Row 1: the one external Owner step

Section 15.2 says that a root whose pool files are all zero "may be removed,
with any `<PROVISION_PATH>.tmp`, and provisioning repeated". The store
implements and tests the repetition (`r3x5`): R-REPUBLISH when a
`<PROVISION_PATH>.tmp` was left, and otherwise provisioning at a new root,
whose gate admits the interrupted root because its pool files are all zero.
Removing the interrupted root stays the Owner's own step:

- **It is optional.** The design says "may". Provisioning at a new root needs
  no removal, and a leftover `<PROVISION_PATH>.tmp` exists only once the
  root is complete and synced, when R-REPUBLISH removes it and publishes.
- **No authority exists for it inside the store.** Before a `PROVISION`
  exists there is no store and no session: no lock to hold, no selection to
  revalidate, nothing to verify after. A store procedure would have to
  decide from a path alone, which the store never does.
- **Design section 15.3 names no such procedure.** Adding one would be a
  design change, which this module does not make.

The interrupted root then stays under the parent, unreferenced and with no
history. If a later succession of the store is interrupted, R-SUCCESSOR
could remove it, under that procedure's own checks.

## Rows R3 changed

- **Row 4** had no procedure at the base: a split revocation stayed Invalid,
  and a second revocation made it worse (R3-B5).
- **Row 6** could not be followed at the base. The repeat failed at step
  2a's `link` (R3-B1, R3-B8), no procedure could remove an incomplete root
  (R3-B2), and a mismatched copy was found only by a failing `link` (R3-B4).
- **Rows 1 and 5** were implemented in R2 but not tested over their crash
  model. R3 tests them (`r3x5`, `r3x6`) without changing their procedures.
