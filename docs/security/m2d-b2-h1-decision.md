# M2d-B2 H1 decision: Hands session addendum

The historical H1 decision is preserved. This host-only addendum follows the
[frozen session specification](m2d-d-session-spec.md), which supersedes the
supported Hands emergency-resume expectation.

H1 still requires input denial independent of blocked initialization, broker,
coordinator, output and recording. The original session cell engages first.
At most four owned Stop workers run; a fifth event remains a bounded recording
obligation, and free slots drain it. Separate receipts establish ingress,
corroborated native halt, synchronous recording completion and durable/owed/
uncertain recording. Panic, spawn failure, poison and lost results are not
successful recording. All shutdown helpers consume the first three-second
monotonic deadline; timeout is nonzero and retains uncertainty.

Hands offers no native lift, fresh undo or cleanup after emergency. Only
status, evidence and Quit remain, with repeated Stop until sealing. An entered
native call may finish documented internal work. Unscheduled-worker, real
storage-stall, repeated-ingress and owned timeout/release/join controls preserve
H1 without relying on a recovery-resume route. Legacy broker recovery controls
remain outside the Hands call graph.

Automated receipts do not establish installed-binary H1 acceptance. The Owner
procedure in [Run Hands](../../mother/README.md#install-and-record-the-exact-build-owner-procedure)
records the reviewed commit, binary hashes, environment, actual Stop/Ctrl-D
observations and exit status. This addendum does not close C-11, authorize a
merge, advance a phase or claim portable terminal support.
