# Review template

Independent review must bind the exact candidate and inspected artifacts. The implementer may prepare an INCONCLUSIVE status only, not an independent ACCEPTABLE_FOR_NEXT_GATE verdict. Allowed verdicts: ACCEPTABLE_FOR_NEXT_GATE, CHANGES_REQUIRED, BLOCKED, INCONCLUSIVE. Acceptance for a next gate does not close a phase or authorize integration. Unmeasured identities stay UNRECORDED with a reason until the reviewer binds them.
